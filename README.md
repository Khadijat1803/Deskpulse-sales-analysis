# DeskPulse Sales Analysis
An Excel project analysing sales for DeskPulse, a business selling office and tech products across Nigerian cities. Completed as part of the TWDP programme (Cohort 1, Ilorin Innovation Hub).

## What the workbook contains
- **Sales**: cleaned dataset of 45 orders (January to May) with calculated revenue, cost and profit
- **Pivot Tables**: summaries by city, product, category, salesperson, payment method and month
- **Dashboard**: charts showing the key numbers at a glance
- **Business Insights**: written findings and recommendations

## Key numbers
- Total revenue: ₦40,249,900
- Total profit: ₦8,655,900
- Units sold: 559
- Orders: 45

## Key insights
1. Lagos made the highest sales (₦13.87M, about a third of total revenue). Kano made the lowest (₦2.25M).
2. Electronics earned the most revenue (₦22.13M), but Accessories sold far more units (363 vs 89).
3. Card payments brought in the most revenue (47%), ahead of Transfer (32%) and Cash (21%).
4. Tolu is the top seller by revenue, but Emeka earns the most profit.
5. Revenue peaked in May (₦9.22M), with a dip in April when there were only 7 orders.
